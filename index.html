Xufeng Duan

Xufeng Duan
段旭峰

I am a Research Assistant Professor in the Language Processing Lab at the Brain and Mind Institute, The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK).

My research connects human language production and large language models. I combine behavioral, EEG, and fMRI data with computational methods to study the mechanisms of language, and use mechanistic interpretability to understand how computations such as syntax and prediction emerge inside LLMs.

Curated Research

Do Large Language Models Acquire Phrase-Based Processing?

Eye tracking, fMRI, and model–brain alignment show phrase-level processing in LLMs and stronger alignment after phrase-segmentation fine-tuning.

10.18653/v1/2026.scil-main.44
Unveiling Language Competence Neurons: A Psycholinguistic Approach to Model Interpretability

Identifying interpretable units in large language models aligned with psycholinguistic effects, bridging model neurons and human linguistic competence.

2025.coling-main.677
MacBehaviour: An R package for behavioural experimentation on large language models

An open-source R toolkit to help researchers quickly set up behavioral experiments interacting with LLMs, either locally or in the cloud.

10.3758/s13428-024-02524-y
